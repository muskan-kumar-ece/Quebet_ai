## 2. Integrity Digest & Auditability Guarantees
- **Integrity Digest (SHA-256)**: Canonical hashes of input parameters and deterministic execution outputs provide verifiable proof of execution context and non-tampering. Note: SHA-256 is an integrity digest and audit trail, not cryptographic authorization.
- **Context Snapshot Binding**: Links execution directly to the canonical context snapshot digest taken at planning time.
- **AI Labeling & Disclaimers**: All explanations produced by `EngineeringExplanationService` carry the mandatory label:
  `ORIGIN: AI_GENERATED (INFORMATIONAL ONLY)`
  and explicit disclaimer:
  `Notice: This analysis is generated by an artificial intelligence copilot for informational purposes only. It does not constitute authoritative engineering sign-off, regulatory approval, or authoritative decisions. Decision authority belongs solely to the authorized human engineer.`
